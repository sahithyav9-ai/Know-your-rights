Why this exists

Confusion is expensive

When people don't know their rights, landlords keep deposits they owe back, employers skip dues they're legally required to pay, and a two-minute police stop turns into hours of fear over rights that were theirs all along. This isn't a law degree — it's the baseline knowledge that lets you recognise when something is wrong and say so with confidence.
